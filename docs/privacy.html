Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Book Tracker ("we", "our", or "the app") handles your personal data. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your personal library data remains completely under your control.

1. Information We Collect

Book Tracker is an offline-first application. All your reading logs, book details, quotes, custom tags, and covers are stored locally on your device by default.

  • User Account Information: When you connect your Google Account, we only receive your email address and profile name to authenticate your Google Drive access.
  • No Analytics or Tracking: We do not track your app usage, sell your data, or use advertising identifiers.

2. Google Drive Data & AppData Scope

Book Tracker uses the Google Drive API to provide an optional cloud backup and sync service.

  • Restricted AppData Folder: The app only requests access to its own private hidden Application Data folder in your Google Drive (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata).
  • No Access to Personal Files: Book Tracker cannot view, modify, or read any other personal files, photos, or documents stored in your Google Drive.
  • No Third-Party Sharing: Your backup files are transferred directly between your device and Google's servers. No third-party server ever receives or processes your reading data.

3. Data Retention and Deletion

You can delete your data at any time directly within the app by deleting your books or uninstalling the app. If you use Google Drive Sync, you can disconnect your account or delete the backup file directly from your Google Account settings.
